Autonomy Ladder

Not every decision deserves the same autonomy level.

Agentius structures agentic workflows by consequence. Read, draft, and recommend actions can move fast. Irreversible actions, external commitments, sensitive data access, or policy exceptions escalate deterministically.

Level 1 Read and classify
Level 2 Draft and recommend
Level 3 Execute inside policy
Level 4 Escalate before irreversible action
